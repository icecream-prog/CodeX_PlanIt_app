PlanIt is an app we made with the help of AI (Claude), it was designed to plan your tasks and organize your schedule. This app allows users to set timers for future dates, allows for reminders before the timer ends (Ex. 10 mins before, it will play a ringtone), and even customization of the background (Ex. mp4, png, jpeg, and other formats).
It also allows for your tasks to be edited, marked done, duplicated to the next day, and deleted, and is used to keep track of your progress.
This app works on Windows and Android.

Features:

**Smart reminders and alarms**
* Two-stage alerts: A reminder comes X minutes before the schedule, then a loud looping alarm at the exact time.
* Snooze: Snooze from the alarm popup, and the alarm rings again when the snooze ends.
* 10 built-in alarm sounds: Classic Beeps, Digital Clock, Siren, Police Two-Tone, Emergency Pulse, Church Bell, Chime Melody, Morning Melody, Buzzer and Sonar Ping. The app generates them itself, so there are no audio files to carry around.
* Sound control: Pick a sound per schedule, or set a default for alarms and a separate one for reminders. Settings also has volume, how long the alarm rings (30 seconds to “until I stop it”) and snooze length.


**Easy to read at a glance**
* Live clock and “Next up” line in the header.
* Live countdown on every card: counts down in real time “in 2h 15m” or “overdue 5m”.
* Colour-coded status: Upcoming, Due soon, Overdue and Done.
* Filters with counts: All, Today, Upcoming, Overdue and Done, plus search through titles and notes.


**Quick actions**
* Mark done, edit, and duplicate to the next day.
* Delete with Undo: A snackbar appears after you delete, so an accidental tap isn’t permanent (you can restore it).
* Friendly checks: It won’t save an empty title, a bad date or time, or a time in the past. It re-arms the alarms if you change a schedule’s time.
* Pick a date and time with calendar and clock pickers, or type them in.


**Customization**
* Any background: Choose a picture (png, jpg, webp, gif, bmp) or a video (mp4, mov, webm and more). The app keeps its own copy.
* Readability controls: A Darken background slider and a Panel opacity slider.
* Responsive layout: The form and list sit side by side on a PC, and switch to bottom tabs on a phone.
* Private and offline: Everything saves on your own device in simple files, with no account or internet needed.


HOW TO RUN:
1. For Windows, extract the zip file, then go in "PlanIt(WINDOWS)", after that scroll down to find the "PlanIt.exe" file and double-click it to run.
2. For Android, extract the zip file, then go in "PlanIt(ANDROID)", after that scroll down to find the "PlanIt.apk" file, then install and open it after installation.


**LIMITATIONS!**
1. Alarms ring only while the app is open, so on a phone it won’t wake a sleeping or closed app.
2. Alarms can ring while outside the app, however, it doesn't have a notification.
3. We only tested this app in Windows and Android, we are not sure how it will work in IOS, MacBook, Linux, and other systems.
